4. BULLETS — length mix per role (word-count; interleave 1/2/3-line; one period only, never ".."):
   - 3–5 bullets @ 35–48 words
   - ≤3 bullets @ 12–18 words
   - rest @ 22–32 words
   MERGE until the mix is met.
5. ASCII only. No extra keys. Finish the full JSON in one reply.
